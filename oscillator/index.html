[ The Label ]

Built for the floor first

Oscillator is an independent techno label founded by KEYV. It exists for records that are built for the floor first: stripped, loud and unpolished, and made to be played loud.

The mix series is the other half of that: a standing invitation to the artists we want people to hear, numbered and archived, no gatekeeping.
