    We are Oscillator

    An independent techno label founded by KEYV, putting one artist forward at a time. Sessions go out numbered and stay up — the archive is kept whole, oldest as long as newest.

    Sound and artwork are made together and released together. The sleeve is the other half of the mix, not a wrapper for it.
